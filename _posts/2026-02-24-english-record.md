# 问答

## Do you like making plans? 

Well, it really depends on the situation. For **important work**, I definitely like making plans because they help me stay organized and reduce stress from all the uncertainty. Having a clear plan means I can **meet deadlines** and usually get better results. 

But when I travel alone, I prefer to be **spontaneous**. Planning every little  detail feels like a waste of time, it might spoil the surprise. However, for group trips, a basic plan is essential，I don't want to waste time in deciding where to go during the journey.


## If you could live in any country right now, where would you went to go and why?
> 虚拟语气

If I could live in any country right now, I would definitely choose **Australia**. 

The main reason is that I have spent my entire life in the **Northern Hemisphere**. As a result, I am very curious to breathe the air of the **Southern Hemisphere** and experience its "seasonal reversal." For example, the idea of celebrating a warm, sunny Christmas sounds like a fascinating adventure that I’ve never had before.

**In my current role as a frontend developer at TikTok Shop**, my daily life is quite fast-paced and high-pressure. I’ve heard that Australia offers a much better **work-life balance** and a more laid-back lifestyle. I think this environment would help me stay creative and reduce the stress from coding all day. 

Even though I am a big fan of **Chinese food**, I’m not too worried about the transition. Australia has a very diverse food scene with many authentic Chinatowns. So, I could still enjoy my favorite Sichuan dishes while exploring the stunning natural landscapes on my own. Overall, I believe living there would broaden my horizons and offer me a fresh perspective on life.


## What would you like to do if you had a day off tomorrow? 

If I had a day off tomorrow, I would definitely use it to **unwind**. 
First, I would sleep in without an alarm. This is the best way to **recharge my energy**.
I would like to do yoga in the afternoon. My life is busy, so yoga can help me find my **inner peace** and strengthen my body. One of the major **benefits** I experienced is it **improved** my **posture** and reduced back pain. And then after doing yoga, I would prepare a nutritious meal, such as a light salad with fresh vegetables. I think healthy food can keep me energetic in the evening. 
In the evening, I want to go shopping. I don’t need to buy much stuff. I just enjoy looking at novel and interesting things.
This day would make me feel relaxed and happy. 


# 高频句

## 第一章：会议与沟通 (Meetings & Communication)
### 场景 1.1: 预约会议 (Scheduling & Invites)
	•	Are you available tomorrow afternoon? (你明天下午有空吗？)
	•	What time works best for you? (你什么时间最方便？)
	•	How about 3 PM in Meeting Room 101? (下午3点在101会议室怎么样？)
	•	Can we find 30 minutes to sync on this? (我们能找30分钟同步一下这个吗？)
	•	I'll send you a Lark calendar invite. (我发你一个飞书日历邀请。)
	•	Please accept the meeting invite so I know you're coming. (请接受一下会议邀请，这样我就知道你会来。)
	•	I've just updated the meeting description. (我刚更新了会议描述。)
	•	I'm adding (xxx) to this meeting. (我把xx也加到这个会议里。)
	•	This meeting is optional for you. (这个会议你可以选择是否参加。)
	•	Sorry, I have a conflict at that time. (抱歉，那个时间我有冲突了。)
	•	Can we reschedule? (我们能改个时间吗？)
	•	I have to decline the meeting. Could you send me the meeting minutes? (我得拒绝这个会了。你能发我一下会议纪要吗？)
	•	Let's make this a recurring meeting. (我们把这个设成一个定期会议吧。)
	•	I'll book a meeting room. (我去预订一个会议室。)
	•	I'm running 5 minutes late. (我会迟到5分钟。)
	•	I'm afraid I have to push back our meeting. (我恐怕得推迟一下我们的会议。)
	•	I'll follow up with an invite. (我稍后会跟进一个邀请。)
	•	I've added (xxx) as an optional attendee. (我把xx加为可选参会人了。)
	•	Could you add the agenda doc to the invite? (你能把议程文档加到邀请里吗？)
	•	Let's find a time slot on the calendar. (我们去日历上找个空闲时段吧。)

### 场景 1.2: 会议开场 (Kick-off & Agenda)
	•	Let's give it a minute or two for more people to join. (我们等一两分钟，让更多人加入。)
	•	In the meantime, feel free to read the doc. ( 在等的时候，大家可以先看一下文档。)
	•	Let's get started. (我们开始吧。)
	•	Is everyone on the call? (都上线了吗？)
	•	You can find the agenda in the Lark doc I shared. (你可以在我分享的飞书文档里找到议程。)
	•	The main goal of this meeting is to... (本次会议的主要目标是...)
	•	We have a lot to cover, so let's get started. (我们有很多内容要讨论，所以开始吧。)
	•	First, let's review the to-do items from the last meeting. (首先，我们回顾一下上次的待办事项。)
	•	The objective today is to align on the new features. (今天的目标是对齐新功能。)
	•	(XXX), could you kick us off? (xx，你能带我们开始吗？)
	•	We have three topics to discuss today. (我们今天有三个议题要讨论。)
	•	Please, let's stick to the agenda. (请大家紧跟议程。)
	•	This meeting should take about an hour. (这个会议大概需要一个小时。)
	•	If you have questions, please hold them until the end. (如果你有问题，请留到最后。)
	•	Let's do a quick round of introductions. (我们来快速轮流介绍一下自己。)
	•	Thanks everyone for making the time. (感谢大家抽出时间。)
	•	The key issue we need to discuss is... (我们需要讨论的关键问题是...)
	•	I've put the key talking points in the invite. (我把主要讨论点放在邀请里了。)
	•	Let's make sure we stay on track. (我们确保不要跑题。)
	•	(xxx) will be facilitating today's meeting. (xx将主持今天的会议。)

### 场景 1.4: 推进讨论 (Facilitating Discussion)
	•	(xxx), what are your thoughts on this? (xx，你对这个有何看法？)
	•	Does anyone have anything to add? (有人有要补充的吗？)

	•	Moving on to the next topic. (继续下一个议题。)
	•	We're running short on time, so let's move on. (我们时间不太够了，所以继续吧。)
	•	That's a good point. Let's take this offline. (这点很好。我们私下讨论。)
	•	Let's circle back to this point later. (我们稍后再回过头来讨论这点。)
	•	To be mindful of everyone's time, let's wrap this part up. (为了不耽误大家的时间，我们结束这部分吧。)
	•	Let's try to stay on topic. (我们尽量不跑题。)
	•	Can you elaborate on that point? (你能详细说明那一点吗？)
	•	Just to clarify, are you saying that...? (只是澄清一下，你的意思是说...？)
	•	Are we all on the same page? (我们都达成一致了吗？)

	•	What's the bottleneck here? (这里的瓶颈是什么？)

	•	Let's put it to a vote. (我们投票表决吧。)
	•	Any objections? (有反对意见吗？)
	•	We seem to have reached a consensus. (我们似乎已经达成了共识。)
	•	What's the root cause of this issue? (这个问题的根本原因是什么？)

### 场景 1.5: 表达观点 (Giving Opinions)

	•	I agree with (xxx). (我同意xx的看法。)
	•	That's a good point. (这一点很好。)
	•	I'm aligned with you on this. (在这一点上我和你一致。)
	•	I see your point, but... (我明白你的意思，但是...)
	•	I see it differently. (我的看法不同。)
	•	I have some concerns about this approach. (我对这个方法有一些顾虑。)
	•	From my perspective,... (从我的角度来看，...)
	•	Sorry to interrupt, but... (抱歉打断一下，但是...)
	•	Can I add something here? (我能补充一点吗？)
	•	Just to add on to what (xx) said... (补充一下xx刚说的...)
	•	Could you give me an example? (你能给我举个例子吗？)
	•	Sorry, I don't quite follow. (抱歉，我没太跟上。)
	•	If I understand correctly, you mean... (如果我理解得没错，你的意思是...)
	•	I support this proposal. (我支持这个提议。)
	•	I'm not sure that's the best idea. (我不确定这是最好的主意。)
	•	To be honest,... (老实说，...)
	•	What if we try another way? (我们换个方式怎么样？)
	•	I'd like to propose a solution. (我想提一个解决方案。)
	•	I'm in favor of Option A. (我赞成A方案。)

### 场景 1.6: 总结与待办事项 (Wrap-up & Action Items)
	•	Okay, let's wrap up. (好了，我们总结一下。)
	•	To recap, we've decided to... (总而言之，我们决定...)
	•	What are the key takeaways? (关键的结论是什么？)
	•	Let's review the Action Items. (我们来过一下待办事项。)
	•	What are the next steps? (下一步是什么？)
	•	Who is the owner for this? (这个的负责人是谁？)
	•	I'll take this one. (这个我来跟。)
	•	(xxx), can you follow up on this? (xx，你能跟进一下这个吗？)
	•	Let's set a deadline for these action items. (我们给这些待办事项设个截止日期。)
	•	When is the due date? (截止日期是什么时候？)
	•	I'll send out the meeting minutes (or Lark doc) later. (我稍后会发出会议纪要[或飞书文档]。)
	•	Let's schedule a follow-up meeting. (我们安排一次跟进会议。)
	•	I think we covered everything. (我想我们都讨论到了。)
	•	Are we missing anything? (我们漏了什么吗？)
	•	Thanks everyone for your time and input. (感谢大家的时间和意见。)
	•	If there's nothing else, we can end here. (如果没别的，我们就到这。)
	•	The conclusion is... (结论是...)
	•	We'll sync up offline on the details. (我们私下再同步细节。)
	•	Let's make sure we are all aligned before we leave. (我们确保在结束前都达成一致了。)
	•	Great meeting. Talk to you soon. (会议很棒。回头聊。)


## 第二章：项目与执行 (Projects & Execution)
### 场景 2.1: 项目启动 (Project Kick-off)
	•	What is the main objective of this project? (这个项目的主要目标是什么？)
	•	We need to define the scope clearly. (我们需要清晰地定义项目范围。)
	•	This is out of scope. (这超出了范围。)
	•	What's the timeline and deadline for this? (这个的时间表和截止日期是什么？)
	•	Let's set some milestones. (我们来设置一些里程碑。)
	•	Who are the key stakeholders? (关键的相关方有哪些？)
	•	What are the deliverables? (需要交付的成果是什么？)
	•	We need to allocate resources properly. (我们需要合理分配资源。)
	•	What is the priority of this project? (这个项目的优先级是？)
	•	This is a P0 (high-priority) task. (这是一个 P0 (高优先级) 的任务。)
	•	Let's draft a project plan. (我们来起草一份项目计划。)
	•	We need to align on the goals first. (我们首先需要对齐目标。)
	•	What are the success metrics (成功指标是什么？)
	•	Who will be the owner for this project? (谁来做这个项目的负责人？)
	•	Let's form a working group. (我们成立一个工作组吧。)
	•	What are the potential risks? (有哪些潜在风险？)
	•	We need to get buy-in from the leadership. (我们需要获得领导层的支持。)
	•	Let's agree on the timeline. (我们对时间表达成一致吧。)
	•	What are the potential blockers? (这个项目有哪些潜在卡点？)
	•	We'll track this project using this dashboard. (我们会用这个看板来跟进项目。)

### 场景 2.2: 进度同步 (Progress Update / Sync-up)
	•	What's the status of this task? (这个任务的进展如何？)
	•	Can you give me a quick update? (你能给我简单同步一下吗？)
	•	We are on track. (我们进度正常。)
	•	We are behind schedule. (我们落后于计划了。)
	•	We are ahead of schedule. (我们领先于计划。)
	•	The project is delayed. （项目延期了。）
	•	I've made good progress on the design. (我在设计方面取得了不错的进展。)
	•	I haven't started yet. (我还没开始。)
	•	I'm still working on it. (我还在处理中。)
	•	The project is on hold. (项目暂停了。)
	•	I need more time to finish this. (我需要更多时间来完成这个。)
	•	What's the progress on your side? (你那边的进展怎么样？)
	•	So far, so good. (目前为止，一切都好。)
	•	I'll be done by EOD (End of Day). (我会在下班前完成。)
	•	The ETA (Estimated Time of Arrival) is next Friday. (预计完成时间是下周五。)
	•	The first phase is almost done. (第一阶段快完成了。)
	•	We need to speed up. (我们需要加快速度。)
	•	Let's have a weekly sync-up on this. (我们每周就这个同步一次。)
	•	I'll keep you posted. (我会随时告知你进展。)
	•	Let's check our to-do list. (我们来核对一下待办清单。)

### 场景 2.3: 问题与风险 (Issues & Risks)
	•	We've run into a problem. (我们遇到了一个问题。)
	•	We are facing some blockers. (我们遇到了一些阻碍。)
	•	What's the main bottleneck? (主要瓶颈是什么？)

	•	This is more complicated than I thought. (这比我想的要复杂。)
	•	We found a bug in the code. (我们在代码里发现了一个bug。)
	•	This task is blocked by (xxx). (这个任务被xx卡住了。)

	•	Do you have any suggestions? (你有什么建议吗？)
	•	Let's brainstorm some ideas. (我们头脑风暴一下。)
	•	What's our Plan B? (我们的B计划是什么？)

	•	We need to escalate this issue. (我们需要上浮这个问题。)
	•	Let's break down the problem. (我们把问题拆解一下。)
	•	How can we mitigate this risk? (我们如何减轻这个风险？)
	•	I'm stuck on this task. Can someone help? (我被这个任务卡住了。有人能帮忙吗？)
	•	The deadline is too tight. (截止日期太紧了。)

	•	What didn't go well? (哪些做得不好？)
	•	What are the lessons learned? (我们学到了哪些经验教训？)

	•	We missed the deadline. (我们错过了截止日期。)
	•	The key takeaway is... (主要的收获是...)
	•	I'll send out the project wrap-up report. (我会发出项目总结报告。)

	•	(xxx) is our main point of contact (POC) for this. (xx是我们这边主要的接口人(POC)。)
	•	How do we hand over the work to the next team? (我们如何把工作交接给下一个团队？)
	•	We need to sync up on the timeline. (我们需要同步一下时间表。)

	•	We need to find a win-win solution. (我们需要找到一个双赢的方案。)
	•	Let's keep everyone in the loop. (我们让每个人都保持信息同步。)
	•	We need to break the silos. (我们需要打破部门墙。)
	•	This is a joint effort. (这是共同努力的结果。)
	•	There's some friction between the two teams. (两个团队之间有一些摩擦。)
	•	We need to clarify the responsibilities. (我们需要明确职责。)
	•	Let's create a shared doc to track progress. (我们建一个共享文档来追踪进展。)

## 第三章：人才与团队 (Talent & Team)
### 场景 3.1: 招聘与面试 (Hiring & Interviewing)
	•	We have a headcount (HC) for a new designer. (我们有一个新设计师的招聘名额(HC)。)
	•	Can you share the Job Description (JD) with me? (你能分享一下职位描述(JD)给我吗？)
	•	The candidate pool looks strong. ( 候选人库看起来很强。)
	•	I've sourced a few interesting candidates. (我搜寻到了一些有意思的候选人。)
	•	Let's schedule an interview with this one. (我们给这位安排一次面试吧。)
	•	Who will be on the interview panel? ( 面试官小组都有谁？)
	•	Can you walk me through your resume? (你能带我过一下你的简历吗？)
	•	What makes you a good fit for ByteDance? (你认为你为什么适合字节跳动？)
	•	This role aligns with my career goals. (这个职位和我的职业目标相契合。)
	•	Can you give me an example of how you handled a conflict? (你能举个例子说明你如何处理冲突吗？)
	•	What's your biggest achievement? (你最大的成就是什么？)
	•	Do you have any questions for me? (你有什么问题想问我吗？)
	•	The interviewer was very professional. (面试官非常专业。)
	•	What's the feedback on this candidate? (对这个候选人的反馈是什么？)

	•	(xxx) is not a good fit for this role. (xx不太适合这个职位。)

# 研发高频句

## 第一章：需求沟通与澄清 (Requirements & Clarification)
### 场景 1.1: 理解业务目标 (Understanding Business Goals)

	•	Could you walk me through the user story for this feature? (能带我过一下这个功能的用户故事吗？)

	•	What is the business goal or the problem we're trying to solve here? (我们想要达成的业务目标，或者说想要解决的问题是什么？)

	•	Who are the target users for this new functionality? (这个新功能的目标用户是谁？)

	•	How will we measure the success of this feature? (我们将如何衡量这个功能的成功？)

	•	What's the priority of this task compared to others in the backlog? (和待办列表里的其他任务相比，这个任务的优先级是怎样的？)

	•	I'm trying to get a better handle on the requirements. (我正试着更好地把握需求。)

	•	What are the key performance indicators (KPIs) for this project? (这个项目的关键绩效指标(KPI)是什么？)

	•	What's the end game for this feature? (这个功能的最终目标是什么？)

### 场景 1.2: 提问与澄清 (Asking for Clarification)
	•	Just to clarify, are we saying that...? (澄清一下，我们是说……吗？)

	•	Could you elaborate on that point? (可以详细阐述一下那一点吗？)

	•	What should be the expected behavior if the user enters invalid data? (如果用户输入了无效数据，预期的行为应该是什么？)

	•	Could you provide a mockup or a design spec for this? (能提供一个设计稿或者设计规格说明吗？)

	•	What are the acceptance criteria (AC) for this story? (这个用户故事的验收标准（AC）是什么？)

	•	Let me rephrase that to make sure I understood correctly. (我换种方式复述一下，确保我的理解是正确的。)

	•	Is this a hard requirement, or is it nice to have? (这是一个硬性要求，还是一个“有了更好”的选项？)

	•	What's the happy path for this user journey? (这个用户流程的理想路径是怎样的？)

	•	We should also consider the unhappy paths, like network errors or invalid inputs. (我们也应该考虑异常路径，比如网络错误或无效输入。)

	•	There's a lot to unpack here. Let's break it down. (这里面信息量很大。我们把它分解一下吧。)

	•	The definition of 'done' for this task is unclear. (这个任务的“完成”定义不清晰。)

	•	This is more of a nice-to-have than a must-have. (这更像是一个“有了更好”的功能，而不是“必须有”的。)

### 场景 1.3: 技术可行性评估 (Technical Feasibility Assessment)
	•	From a technical standpoint, this looks feasible. (从技术角度来看，这看起来是可行的。)

	•	This might be challenging due to our current architecture. (考虑到我们现有的架构，这个实现起来可能会有挑战。)

	•	Let's do a quick sanity check before we proceed. (在我们继续之前，先来快速做一个合理性检查。)

### 场景 1.4: 工作量评估 (Effort Estimation)
	•	My rough estimate for this would be around 3 to 5 story points. (我对这个任务的粗略估算大概是3到5个故事点。)

	•	Could you provide a ballpark estimate? (你能给一个大概的估算吗？)

	•	What's the level of effort (LOE) for this task? (这个任务的工作量级是多少？)

	•	This task has a lot of moving parts. (这个任务牵涉到很多变动的环节。)

	•	The scope of this story seems too large for one sprint. Can we split it? (这个用户故事的范围对于一个冲刺来说似乎太大了。我们能拆分它吗？)
